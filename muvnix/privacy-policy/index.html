Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MUVNIX handles information when you use the app to discover movies and TV shows.

Effective date: November 26, 2026 Last updated: November 26, 2026
MUVNIX does not require an account or ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number, or password. The app retrieves entertainment information from third-party services, as explained below.

1. About this policy

MUVNIX is a movie and TV-show discovery application developed by Sarmad Labs. This Privacy Policy describes the information handled when you use the app and explains the role of third-party services used to provide its content.

2. Information you provide

MUVNIX does not currently offer account registration, user profiles, messaging, or user-submitted reviews. It does not ask you to provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Based on the app functionality currently described, Sarmad Labs does not operate a user account or personal-profile database for MUVNIX.

3. Third-party services and connection information

The Movie Database (TMDB). MUVNIX makes requests to the TMDB API to retrieve movie, TV-show, cast, crew, rating, genre, release-date, description, and related entertainment information. When your device connects to TMDB, TMDB may receive technical connection information such as your IP address, request details, and information normally included in an internet request. TMDB handles information it receives under its own policies.

Read the TMDB Privacy Policy.

Google Fonts. MUVNIX includes the google_fonts Flutter package. If a font used by the app is not bundled locally and is instead fetched at runtime, the device may request that font from Google Fonts. In that case, Google may receive technical connection information associated with the request. Fonts bundled within the app do not require that runtime font request.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and the google_fonts package documentation.

4. Local caching

The app includes components capable of caching images and storing data locally on your device. This may be used to improve loading and browsing performance. Local cache data is stored on your device rather than in a MUVNIX user account. You can generally clear app-stored data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app; the exact behavior depends on your device and operating system.

5. How information is used

Information involved in network requests is used to retrieve and display the entertainment content you request, load related images and fonts where applicable, and support the basic operation and performance of the app.

6. Sharing and sale of personal information

Sarmad Labs does not sell personal information. MUVNIX sends requests to third-party content and font services as described in Section 3 so that the app can display its features. Those providers process the connection information they receive under their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

MUVNIX does not currently maintain user accounts or a developer-operated database of user profile information. Temporary or cached content may remain on your device until it is cleared by the app, your device settings, or app uninstallation. Any information retained by third-party providers is governed by their own retention practices and policies.

8. Security

MUVNIX uses network connections to retrieve content from third-party services. We take reasonable care in developing and maintaining the app, but no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

MUVNIX does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Sarmad Labs in connection with MUVNIX, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, integrations, or information-handling practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective or revision date.
